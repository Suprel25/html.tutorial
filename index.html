About

Front End Simplified (FES) is an online learning platform designed to help aspiring developers build the skills needed for modern web development careers. Through structured lessons, hands-on projects, mentorship, and career guidance, FES teaches essential technologies such as HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, and more. The program focuses on helping students gain practical experience, build professional portfolios, and prepare for opportunities in the tech industry.
